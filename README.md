# 🚗 Vehicle Management System

A web-based **Vehicle Management System** developed to manage the day-to-day activities of a vehicle body building and service workshop.

The system replaces manual record keeping with a centralized database-driven application that helps manage companies, vehicles, employees, services, work assignments, quotations, salaries, and reports.

## 📌 Project Overview

The Vehicle Management System is designed for vehicle body building and service companies where vehicles are received from different customer companies for various types of work.

The system helps maintain vehicle and customer details, track services and work assignments, manage quotations and employee information, and generate useful reports.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Reduce manual record keeping.
- Store workshop information in a centralized database.
- Manage customer companies and their vehicles.
- Maintain employee details.
- Manage different types of vehicle services.
- Track work assignments and vehicle status.
- Manage quotations and salary information.
- Provide reports for better management.
- Make workshop operations easier and more organized.

## ✨ Features

### 🔐 Login System
- Secure login page.
- User authentication.
- Logout functionality.

### 🏢 Company Management
- Add company details.
- View company records.
- Edit company information.
- Delete companies when there are no linked records.

### 🚗 Vehicle Management
- Add vehicle details.
- Associate vehicles with customer companies.
- Track vehicle status.
- Update and delete vehicle records.

### 👨‍🔧 Employee Management
- Store employee information.
- Manage employee details.
- Maintain salary-related information.

### 🔧 Service Management
- Add and manage workshop services.
- Store service details.
- Edit and delete services.

### 🛠️ Work Assignment
- Assign services to vehicles.
- Assign employees to work.
- Track work progress and completion.

### 📄 Quotation Management
- Create quotations for customers.
- Maintain quotation details.
- Manage quotation records.

### 💰 Salary Management
- Maintain employee salary information.
- Manage salary records.

### 📊 Reports
- View useful workshop information.
- Monitor vehicle and work-related records.

### 📱 Dashboard
- Centralized dashboard.
- Quick access to different modules.
- Vehicle/workshop themed interface.
